SoloDesk Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 25, 2026

SoloDesk ("we", "our", or "us") is a mobile application developed by Gadia Digital Services. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our app.

1. Information We Collect

  • Camera & Photos: Used solely to capture and scan receipts. Photos are stored locally on your device and never uploaded to our servers.
  • Receipt Data: Merchant names, amounts, dates, and categories you enter are stored locally on your device using secure on-device storage.
  • Calendar: Used only to create and read work order events you schedule within the app.
  • OCR Processing: When you scan a receipt, the image is sent to Google Cloud Vision API for text recognition. This is processed securely and not stored by us.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide receipt scanning, expense tracking, invoicing, and mileage logging features.
  • To auto-fill receipt fields using OCR text recognition.
  • To generate reports and exports you request.

3. Data Storage

All your data (receipts, invoices, clients, mileage trips) is stored locally on your device. We do not operate servers that store your personal data. You are in full control of your data at all times.

4. Third-Party Services

  • Google Cloud Vision API: Used for receipt OCR. Images are transmitted securely and subject to Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Expo / React Native: The app framework used to build SoloDesk.

5. Permissions

  • Camera: To scan receipts.
  • Photo Library: To select existing images for receipts.
  • Calendar: To schedule and view work orders.
  • Notifications: To send invoice due date and work order reminders.

6. Children's Privacy

SoloDesk is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected by the "Last updated" date above.
